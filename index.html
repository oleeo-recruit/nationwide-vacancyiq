How it works

From a hiring need
to a draft you can build on.

No blank page. Just focused questions to help you explain the role, bring the details together and create content that’s ready for your review. You stay in control at every step.

  1. 01

    Tell us about the role

    Share its purpose, day-to-day responsibilities and the skills you need. Add the practical details that help candidates decide whether it’s right for them.

  2. 02

    Bring the content together

    Build a connected set of recruitment materials from one conversation, so the job advert, role requirements and questions tell the same story.

  3. 03

    Make it right, together

    Check the facts, refine the wording and agree the final content with the right colleagues before you share it with candidates.
